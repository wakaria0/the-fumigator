About The Fumigator

Trusted pest elimination expertise since 2020.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, The Fumigator started as a small family operation with a single van and a commitment to doing the job right. Over the years, we've grown into one of the region's most trusted fumigation and pest control companies, treating thousands of homes and businesses annually.

Along the way, our customers asked for more — so we expanded into professional sofa set cleaning, mattress cleaning and carpet cleaning, becoming a one-stop solution for a pest-free and spotlessly clean home or workplace.

What hasn't changed is our dedication to safety, thoroughness, and customer satisfaction. Every technician on our team undergoes continuous training to stay current with the latest industry standards and techniques.

Our Mission

To provide safe, effective, and environmentally responsible fumigation and pest control solutions for all residential and commercial clients, alongside top-quality sofa set, mattress and carpet cleaning that protect our clients' properties and give them peace of mind.
