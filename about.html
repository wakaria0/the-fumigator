About The Fumigator

Over a decade of proven pest elimination expertise.

Our Story

Founded in 2011, The Fumigator started as a small family operation with a single van and a commitment to doing the job right. Over the years, we've grown into one of the region's most trusted fumigation companies, treating thousands of properties annually.

What hasn't changed is our dedication to safety, thoroughness, and customer satisfaction. Every technician on our team is fully certified and undergoes continuous training to stay current with the latest industry standards and techniques.

Our Mission

To provide safe, effective, and environmentally responsible pest control solutions that protect our clients' properties and give them peace of mind.

Why We're Different
